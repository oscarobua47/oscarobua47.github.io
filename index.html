🤖 What Is Ashlar?

Ashlar is a revolutionary tool that connects your AI assistant directly to your Minecraft Paper server. Imagine telling ChatGPT to build a castle, and it actually does it – block by block, right in your live world. This is not a mod; it's a bridge between artificial intelligence and your Minecraft server.

Whether you use Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or any other AI agent, Ashlar acts as a universal translator. It lets these AIs see your server, understand the terrain, construct structures, and inspect the results in real-time.

⚡ How It Works

Ashlar uses Model Context Protocol (MCP) – a standard way for AI to interact with software. Here's what happens:

  1. 👁️ Survey: The AI explores your server, mapping blocks, terrain, and structures.
  2. 🎨 Render: It visualizes the World – understanding dimensions, layouts, and building materials.
  3. 🏗️ Build: The AI executes commands to place, remove, or modify blocks anywhere on the server.
  4. 🔍 Inspect: After building, it reviews the work, checks for errors, and fine-tunes details.

It's like having a master builder who can also read your mind – except it's an AI that follows your instructions perfectly.
